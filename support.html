Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQs
You can track your order by entering your Order ID on the "Track Your Order" card above. Additionally, a shipment tracking link will be sent to your registered email address and phone number as soon as the package is shipped.
We offer a 30-day return policy for all unused products in their original packaging. Simply go to the returns portal inside your account page, select the items you wish to return, and print your free prepaid shipping label.
Once your returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, we will initiate a refund to your original payment method. The refund processing typically takes 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank.
We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), UPI payments, Net Banking, Apple Pay, PayPal, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for selected regions.
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping delivery is completed within 1-2 business days. Delivery timelines may vary slightly based on product availability and shipping address.
